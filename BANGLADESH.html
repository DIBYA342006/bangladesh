Stop 2 • Brave Words

The Magic of Our Mother Language (1952) 🗣️

Imagine if someone told you that you were never allowed to speak your favorite language again!

🍯

Sweet Bangla Words

Bengali people loved their language, Bangla. It was the language of bedtime songs, grandmother stories, and lullabies.

🚫

The Unfair Rule

Rulers far away said: "Only Urdu will be the state language!" But more than half the people spoke Bangla! They said: "No, we want our mother language recognized too!"

🌹

February 21, 1952

Brave university students marched with posters of Bangla letters. They stood tall so the language could live forever. Now, UNESCO celebrates February 21 as International Mother Language Day for every country on Earth!

Stop 3 • The Big Voice

The Big Election & The Racecourse Thunder 📢

When millions of people vote, fairness says you listen. But the rulers didn't want to listen!

🗳️

1. The People Voted!

In December 1970, people lined up peacefully. Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman won an enormous democratic victory (167 out of 169 seats!). But the generals refused to hand over leadership.

⚡

2. March 7, 1971: The Big Speech!

Two million people gathered at the Racecourse field in Dhaka. Bangabandhu stepped up in his black vest and spoke with thunderous courage:

"Build a fortress in every house! Turn everything you have into resistance!"

UNESCO recognizes this speech as a world treasure!
